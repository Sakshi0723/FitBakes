Are You A Diabetic Person ?

Tips for Eating Cake with Diabetes:

  • Portion Control is Key: Have a tiny slice or share with a friend;don't feel deprived, enjoy just a few bites mindfully.
  • Read Labels: Check total carbs, sugar, and serving sizes to understand impact.
  • Mindful Eating: Savor each bite, focusing on taste and texture, ideally after a meal to slow sugar absorption.
  • Occasional Indulgence: Cake is for special occasions, not daily consumption.
Healthier Cake Options & Swaps:
  • Use almond flour, coconut flour, or whole wheat/oats instead of white flour.
  • Sweeteners: Use erythritol, stevia, or monk fruit instead of sugar.
  • Fats: Swap butter for rapeseed oil or mashed banana in recipes.
  • Add-ins: Berries add flavor and fiber; dark chocolate (high cocoa) is better than milk chocolate.
