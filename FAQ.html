FAQ'S

Can I customize my cake?

Yes! FitBakes allows you to customize cakes based on your flavor, weight, occasion, and special message. You can also upload a reference image for your custom design.

Do you provide healthy or sugar-free cakes?

Yes. FitBakes offers health-friendly cakes such as:

  • Sugar-free cakes
  • Oats cakes
  • Protein cakes
  • Low-sugar cakes
These cakes are suitable for diabetic or fitness-conscious customers.

How many days before should I order a custom cake?

We recommend placing custom cake orders at least 1–2 days in advance. For complex designs, 3–5 days notice may be required.

What is your cancellation policy?

Custom cake orders can be canceled within 24 hours of placing the order for a full refund. After 24 hours, cancellations may be subject to a fee depending on the stage of production.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we offer delivery within a 10-mile radius of our bakery. Delivery fees may apply based on distance.

Can I request a specific design or theme for my cake?

Yes, you can request specific designs or themes for your cake. Please provide details and reference images when placing your order.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes, we can accommodate various dietary restrictions such as gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free. Please specify your requirements when ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, PayPal, and cash on delivery for local orders.

How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our customer support team via email at support@fitbakes.com or by phone at +91 9876543210. We are available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM.

Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?

Yes, we offer discounts for bulk orders. Please contact our sales team for more information on pricing and discounts for large orders.
